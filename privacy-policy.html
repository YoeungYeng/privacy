Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026 | Effective Date: October 5, 2026
Overview: MetaBot Studio ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency. This Privacy Policy details how our application collects, uses, handles, and protects information when you access our platform or connect your Facebook Pages and accounts.

1. Information We Collect

When you use MetaBot Studio, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you authenticate via Facebook Login, we collect your public Facebook User ID, Name, Profile Avatar URL, and Email address (if permitted).
  • Facebook Pages Data: With your explicit consent via Facebook OAuth permissions (pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_messaging, pages_read_engagement), we collect Page Names, Page IDs, Page Profile Pictures, and Page Access Tokens necessary to perform automation.
  • Messaging & Interaction Data: To operate the 24/7 automated chatbot, our system receives webhook notifications containing the Page-Scoped User ID (PSID) of users messaging your page, message text, timestamps, and interactive button payload responses.
  • Post & Media Data: Text, media attachments (images/photos), scheduling timestamps, and post IDs that you upload or compose to be published on Facebook Pages.
  • Log & Technical Data: Server request logs, IP addresses, browser user agent, and operational timestamps generated during standard application usage for troubleshooting and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly utilize the collected data for operational and functional purposes requested by you:

  • To deliver instant automated responses and interactive chatbot flows to your Facebook Page visitors via Messenger.
  • To compose, schedule, publish, and manage feed posts and multimedia content on your connected Facebook Pages.
  • To monitor and reply to customer comments and inquiries on your public Facebook Page posts.
  • To maintain system stability, diagnose errors, prevent fraudulent usage, and ensure technical performance.

3. Meta Platform Terms & Data Protection

We comply fully with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies:

  • No Data Brokering: We NEVER sell, lease, rent, trade, or transfer your personal data, Facebook User data, or Page data to any third-party data brokers or advertising networks.
  • No Surveillance or Profiling: We do not use Facebook data to conduct surveillance, track users across third-party websites, or build discriminatory profiles.
  • Minimal Access: We request only the minimum required OAuth scopes needed to fulfill the core features of our chatbot and scheduling service.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and electronic security safeguards:

  • All communications between client devices, our servers, and Meta Graph APIs are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access tokens and confidential database credentials are encrypted and stored in secure, password-protected databases with restricted administrative access.
  • Uploaded media files and scheduled posts are securely isolated on protected server storage.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

In full adherence to Meta Developer Policies, you retain absolute ownership over your data and can request deletion at any time through any of the following methods:

  1. From the Application: Navigate to your Account & Pages tab inside MetaBot Studio and click "Disconnect Page" or "Remove Account". All associated page access tokens and local automation rules will be immediately purged.
  2. Via Facebook Business Integrations: Go to your Facebook Profile > Settings & Privacy > Settings > Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites). Find MetaBot Studio and click Remove. Facebook will automatically dispatch a secure Data Deletion Webhook to our server to erase your linked data.
  3. By Direct Email Request: Send an email to support@metabotstudio.com with the subject "User Data Deletion Request" including your Facebook Page ID or Account Name. We guarantee the permanent deletion of your data within 48 hours and will send you a confirmation confirmation code.

6. Data Retention Policy

We retain your account data and Page access tokens only for as long as your account remains active on MetaBot Studio. If you disconnect your page or terminate your service, all related authentication tokens and scheduled post drafts are permanently deleted from our active databases.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed exclusively for business owners, page administrators, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected, it will be deleted immediately.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our service or evolving legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our application signifies acceptance of any updated terms.
